Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Findorah (“we”, “us”) is a marketplace for stores, restaurants, places to stay, vehicles and property in Zambia. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider) when you create an account.
  • Listing content: titles, descriptions, prices, photos, logos, locations and the phone or WhatsApp numbers that sellers choose to publish. Anything you put in a listing is public.
  • Activity: items you save, reviews you write, reports you submit, and counts of views and contacts on listings (shown to the listing’s seller or manager as totals).
  • Search: recent searches are kept on your own device. Words that return no results may be logged, without your identity, to help us improve the catalogue.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, we store the technical subscription details needed to send them to your device.
  • Device data: basic technical information such as browser type, used to keep the service working and secure.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show listings, search results and saved items.
  • To let buyers contact sellers, and to give sellers and managers statistics about their listings.
  • To review listings and reports, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send notifications you have asked for and important service messages.
  • To improve Findorah.

What is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Listing details, including any phone number a seller publishes, are visible to everyone. When you tap a WhatsApp or call button, you leave Findorah and that service’s own policy applies. We use trusted providers to host the app, store data and images, and handle sign-in (currently Supabase). They process data only to provide those services. We may disclose information when the law requires it or to protect users and the service.

Managers

An administrator can appoint a manager for a store, restaurant or place to stay using the manager’s email address. Managers can edit that listing and its products. They cannot delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account and listing data while your account or listing is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for security and legal reasons. To delete your account or a listing, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to certain uses. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time. We handle personal data in line with applicable Zambian law, including the Data Protection Act, 2021.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Findorah is not meant for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of Findorah after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See our Contact page.
