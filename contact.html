List your business

Stores, restaurants and places to stay are listed by Findorah. Email us your business name, location, phone number and logo and we will get you set up. We can also appoint a manager (using their email address) so someone from your business can keep the listing and its products up to date.

Report a listing

Open the listing in the app and use Report. If something is urgent, email us with a link to the listing.

Account and privacy requests

To correct or delete your account or data, email us from the address on your account. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
